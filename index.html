Publication

IEEE Transactions on Multimedia · 2025

Robust Visual Food Recognition for Enriching Nutrition Knowledge Bases

Zhaoyan Ming, Zeyu Xie, Chao Zhang, Kui Su, Changzheng Yuan, and Tat-Seng Chua

We use language models to expand food names and develop cAUM and AccGap to address label noise in web images. The resulting food recognition system connects photographs to a nutrition knowledge base.

My contribution. As the first student author, I led the experiments and manuscript writing under my advisor’s supervision. I also led the provincial undergraduate research project behind the paper, contributing to food selection, synonym expansion, and web-image collection.

Current research

Personalized glucose forecasting from meal histories

I lead a National Undergraduate Innovation Project on predicting glucose trajectories over the 90 minutes after a meal. I developed features that summarize each person’s prior meals and glucose responses to personalize forecasts for later meals.

In an internal temporal evaluation covering 10,701 meals from 928 participants, adding these features to a matched TabM baseline reduced MAE by 3.02%, TWED by 2.18%, and observed iAUC error by 6.00%.

Manuscript in preparation.
